ArchSpec 1.2.2 keeps ERB components working after Herb 0.11.2, published today, which breaks them for anyone installing ArchSpec 1.2.0 or 1.2.1 fresh.

## Fixed

- **ERB components no longer fail with `Invalid serialization`.** Herb 0.11.2 writes ERB programs in Prism 2's format, and ArchSpec stays on Prism 1. ArchSpec now requires Herb below 0.11.2. If you've already resolved Herb 0.11.2, `bundle update archspec` moves you back. By @crmne ([#65](https://github.com/crmne/archspec/pull/65)).

## Known limitations

- **ArchSpec stays on Prism 1 and Herb 0.11.1 for now,** so it can't share a bundle with a gem that requires Prism 2.

**Full changelog**: https://github.com/crmne/archspec/compare/v1.2.1...v1.2.2
