ArchSpec 1.2.2 keeps ERB components working after Herb 0.11.2, published today, which breaks them for anyone installing ArchSpec 1.2.0 or 1.2.1 fresh.

## Fixed

- **ERB components no longer fail with `Invalid serialization`.** Herb writes ERB programs in its bundled Prism's format, and Prism loads only its own version's output. Herb 0.11.2 writes Prism 2's, and Herb 0.11.0 and 0.11.1 write Prism 1.9's, so any older Prism failed too. ArchSpec now requires Herb 0.11.0 or 0.11.1 with Prism 1.9. If you've already resolved Herb 0.11.2, `bundle update archspec` moves you back. By @crmne ([#65](https://github.com/crmne/archspec/pull/65)).

## Known limitations

- **ArchSpec can't share a bundle with a gem that requires Prism 2, Herb 0.11.2, or a Prism older than 1.9.** Bundler reports a conflict or keeps an older ArchSpec until that requirement is resolved.

**Full changelog**: https://github.com/crmne/archspec/compare/v1.2.1...v1.2.2
