ArchSpec 1.3 moves to Prism 2 and Herb 0.11.2, so it can share a bundle with the current Herb, ReActionView, RuboCop, and Minitest again. Nothing changes in how you declare or check your architecture.

## New

- **ArchSpec runs on Prism 2 and Herb 0.11.2.** Herb writes ERB programs in its bundled Prism's format, and Prism reads only its own major version's output, so the two move together: ArchSpec now requires Prism 2 with Herb 0.11.2 or a later 0.11 release. Run `bundle update archspec herb prism` to move. By @crmne.

## Fixed

- **An incomplete constant path such as `Foo::` is still a syntax diagnostic on Prism 2,** not a crash. Prism 2 renamed the error it raises for these. By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- **ArchSpec 1.3 can't share a bundle with a gem that requires Prism 1 or Herb 0.11.1 and older.** Stay on ArchSpec 1.2.2 until those gems allow Prism 2; most current releases do, such as rubocop-ast 1.51, minitest 6.1, and flay 2.14.5.
- **Ruby files are parsed with the syntax of the Ruby running ArchSpec,** Prism 2's default, rather than the newest syntax Prism knows. Run ArchSpec on the Ruby your app uses.
